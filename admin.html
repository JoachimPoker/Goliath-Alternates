    🔔 Alert Me Notifications

    Lets players tap "Alert Me" on an active event and get a push notification on their phone when it's their number or past it - works even with the site closed. Turning this off hides the button on the website and stops any notifications from being sent, immediately.

    Sends a real push notification to this device right now, bypassing everything else - a quick way to confirm the whole pipeline (this browser → Supabase → Google/Apple's push service) is actually working, without needing a real subscriber or changing any event's alt number.

    📺 Reg Desk Video Wall

    Toggle each station open/closed and set what it's serving from the Stations panel up top. Updates the video wall instantly. When all 4 are closed, the wall switches to the idle slideshow.

    Live Screen Background

    Behind the header and stations while at least one station is open. Add as many as you like - each has its own display duration. With just one, it shows statically (no rotation). Design at 1920x384 for a clean full-bleed fit. Leave empty to use the default gradient.

    Idle Screen Images

    Shown full-screen when all 4 stations are closed. Add as many as you like - each has its own display duration (images and GIFs) or plays through once per loop (video). With just one, it shows statically. Design at 1920x384 for a clean full-bleed fit.
